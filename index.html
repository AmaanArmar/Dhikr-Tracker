Essential Duas & Zikr Reference

SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi (Full High-Reward Version)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ، وَرِضَا نَفْسِهِ، وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ، وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ
"Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him, by the number of His creation, by His pleasure, by the weight of His Throne, and by the ink of His words."
(Sahih Muslim) — Outweighs hours of continuous morning remembrance when recited 3 times.
Two Beloved & Heavy Phrases on the Scales
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ
"Glory be to Allah and His is the praise, Glory be to Allah the Greatest."
(Sahih Bukhari & Muslim) — Light on the tongue, heavy on the scales, beloved to the Most Merciful.
Dua For Daily Protection (Morning & Evening — 3 Times)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
"In the Name of Allah, with Whose Name nothing can harm on earth or in heaven, and He is the All-Hearing, All-Knowing."
(Sunan Abi Dawud 5088 | Sahih)
Dua For Best in Both Worlds
رَبَّنا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
"Our Lord, give us good in this world and in the Hereafter, and protect us from the torment of the Fire."
(Surah Al-Baqarah: 201)
