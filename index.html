The Four Most Beloved Words (Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah, etc.)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، وَلاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
"Glory be to Allah, Praise be to Allah, There is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest." (Sahih Muslim)
Juwayriyah's High Reward Zikr
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ وَرِضَا نَفْسِهِ وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ
"Glory be to Allah and Praise be to Him, equal to the number of His creation, the pleasure of His Self, the weight of His Throne, and the ink of His words."
Sayyid al-Istighfaar (Master of Forgiveness - Morning & Evening)
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ
"O Allah, You are my Lord, none has the right to be worshipped except You. You created me and I am Your servant..."
Dua for Purification & Piety of the Soul
اللَّهُمَّ آتِ نَفْسِي تَقْوَاهَا، زَكِّهَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا، أَنْتَ وَلِيُّهَا وَمَوْلَاهَا
"O Allah, grant my soul its piety and purify it, for You are the best who purifies it. You are its Guardian and Master." (Sahih Muslim)
